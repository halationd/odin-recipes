Spicy Doritos Chicken

Back to homepage

A nice little twist on breaded chicken.

Ingredients

  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Tablespoon of water
  • 3/4 Cup of crushed doritos
  • 1/3 Cup of breadcrumbs
  • 2 Skinless boneless chicken breasts

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 175C
  2. Beat egg, water, and pepper together in a bowl
  3. Mix doritos, breadcrumbs, and pepper together in a separate bowl
  4. Dip chicken into the egg mixture until coated
  5. Bake in oven for about 25 minutes
