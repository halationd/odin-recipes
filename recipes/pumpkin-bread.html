Pumpkin Bread

Back to homepage

A moist dish full of warm spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 can pumpkin puree
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 3 cups white sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 175C
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and ginger together in a large bowl.
  3. Whisk pumpkin purée, eggs, oil, water, and sugar together in a separate large bowl until smooth and well combined
  4. Stir the flour mixture into the pumpkin mixture just until no dry streaks remain; do not overmix
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of each loaf comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs, about 50 minutes
  6. Cool the loaves in their pans for 10 minutes. Run a thin knife around the edges, then turn the loaves onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing or wrapping
